How It Works

A clear path from first conversation
to finished project.

No surprises. No runaround. We built our process around the things homeowners told us they hated about construction.

01

First Steps Consultation

We start with an honest conversation about your project — scope, timeline, budget, and what you actually need before hiring a contractor.

02

Estimate & Plan

We develop a detailed cost estimate and project plan — so you go into bidding and hiring with full information, not assumptions.

03

Contractor Selection

We help you evaluate bids, vet contractors, and choose the right team — without the pressure or conflicts that come from referral-based hiring.

04

Active Oversight

Once construction begins, we're your on-the-ground eyes. We track progress, catch problems early, and keep everyone accountable to the plan.

Why Watchman

We work for you — not the contractor.

Most construction problems aren't about bad contractors. They're about missing oversight — no one watching the budget, the schedule, or whether the work matches what was agreed to.

Watchman is your independent advocate throughout the entire process. We don't take referral fees, we don't have contractor relationships to protect, and we don't disappear after the first meeting.

No contractor markup We're fee-based and independent — our only incentive is your project going well.
Clear, honest communication You'll know what's happening on your project — good news and bad — in plain language.
