Four steps to a remodel you can trust

A clear process with no guesswork — from your first call to the day you move back in.

01

Discovery Call

We start by understanding your vision, goals, and budget. No pressure — just an honest conversation about what's possible and what it'll take.

02

Plan & Budget

We build a detailed project scope, timeline, and open-book budget before a single contractor is hired. You approve everything before we proceed.

03

Build & Manage

We run daily site oversight, coordinate all trades, handle permits, and send you weekly updates — so you're always informed without being overwhelmed.

04

Final Walkthrough

We do a detailed punch-list inspection, make sure every detail meets your expectations, and hand over your finished space — ready to enjoy.
